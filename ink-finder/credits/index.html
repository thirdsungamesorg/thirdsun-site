Find & Color Credits and licences

Updated 7 November 2026

Find & Color is made by Third Sun Games, Inc. It uses sounds, fonts and software made by other people. This page credits them and gives the licences they ask us to pass on. The Terms of Service cover the game itself.

1. Sounds

Sound effects: "Triumphant success" by mokasza (https://freesound.org/people/mokasza/sounds/810330/) and "Level Up 01" by mokasza (https://freesound.org/people/mokasza/sounds/810753/), available under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The game plays both sounds as they were published, apart from the compression applied when the game is built.
